# ADR 0005: Validate Customer Demand in Parallel With Engineering

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The technical system can be coherent and still miss the buyer. The highest product risk is
not raster math; it is building a risk API without a design partner, letter of intent, or
loss-data access path.

## Decision

Discovery calls with MGAs, brokers, and parametric insurance actors happen in Week 1 and
Week 2 while the core engine is built.

## Consequences

- Engineering decisions should absorb buyer feedback early.
- The public contract may change before hardening.
- A POC without customer validation is considered incomplete, even if tests are green.
